Portfolio Bakery
** You kept the model, but did you keep the  recipe??**
 A simple tool that assists mid to big quant trading teams hand over research properly. When a research leaves or moves to a new project, our bakery keeps all the important context(code, data, assumptions and notes on what didnt't work), alongside important elements such as points of contact, link to codebase) attached to the model, essentially as a hubbase to assits quant trading teams.

 ## The Initital Problem
When a quant researcher builds a trading strategy, the final code is only part of the picture. Thing like which dataset they used, what assumptions they made, what they tried and didn't work, and why. If that person leaves the team, whoever inherits their work is stuck with code they don't fully understand.

 ## Our Assumptions and interviews
 We started off thinking the main problem was merely ownership, that research just sits there with no clear reason once a researcher leaves, resulting in our plan to implement an assitance handover system. Our Research uncovered that small quant teams have the higest risk of "Key person" allocation where if a key researcher leaves, a major crisis results where their research becomes difficult for other members to continue or reproduce without the same environment, versions or execution paths. 
 
 However, after interviewing a Canva engineer, we were driven to udnerstand that our assumption and proposed plan was flawed for small startups. He mentioned that from his personal experince, small startups would not rely as much on a handover website due to small teams already having their process planned out and implicit shared processe, and that turover rate is lower.

 Hence, we shifted our focus towards designing our Bakery to be more applicable to medium to largeer corporations. After having a conversation with the Canva engineer, he suggest a plethora of suggestions that would benefit a team hubbase styled handover site, in order to increase it's "value" within a corporations, including point of contacts, codebases, faq and recordings/notes(resource hub). Though, the engineer did mention that a checklist system would be quite convenient to incorporate inside, and after viewing out site, recommended the prior suggestions.

With discussion, we realsied to elevate our tool's efficeincy and function, major additions had to be implemented. One major implementation was the archive, where older unused models would be stored for futre reference essentially as a resource hub. Another critical addition was github/repostiory links to each codebase outside of our checklist function.
